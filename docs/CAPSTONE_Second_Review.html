Introduction — What is CAPSTONE?

One sentence: a command layer above banks, brokers & CAs — not another finance app.

CAPSTONE unifies an HNI family’s scattered wealth, watches tax risk continuously,
and tells you what to do — without ever trading, lending, or filing ITR.

Sees everything

Family wealth across banks, brokers, entities & CA books in one God Mode screen.

Thinks like a CA desk

Fin — the AI buddy — answers on demand and proposes a daily plan for review.

Never moves money

Challenges — Honest Risks

We design around these; we do not pretend they are solved.

AA / FIU sandbox

Real bank & MF consent depends on Setu Bridge product config and credentials — not only our code.

Tax correctness

Rules change. Mitigated by deterministic calculators + cite-or-refuse RAG — LLM never invents numbers.

Trust & compliance

Hands-off product: no custody/trading. Still need clear RIA path and CA-in-the-loop for desk plans.

Data privacy

HNI PII is sensitive. Consent-first sharing, no plain-text secrets, India-only residency for prod.

Adoption wedge

HNIs are busy; CAs are the channel. Advisor Mesh + fee-only ROI story must be proven with pilots.

Scope discipline

Temptation to become ITR portal / distributor. Anti-goals constitution is the permanent brake.
